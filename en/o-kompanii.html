About the firm

Our core work is Interpol notice removal. The office is in Tbilisi; we assist clients from any country.

What we do

ITP Consulting handles Interpol notice challenges: data checks, CCF filings and support in extradition requests. Alongside that core practice we advise on international protection and relocation to Georgia.
