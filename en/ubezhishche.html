Forms of protection

What status can be sought

Georgia’s Law on International Protection provides several forms. The right one depends on the facts of the case.

Refugee status

For those with a well-founded fear of persecution on grounds such as political opinion, religion or nationality.

Humanitarian status

Where return would expose a person to a real risk of serious harm, but refugee grounds are not met.

Temporary protection

Used in specific cases of mass influx, by a state decision.

What we do

  • Assess grounds and risks before filing
  • Help gather and translate evidence
  • Prepare you for the migration-department interview
  • Attend interviews and watch the deadlines
  • Appeal a refusal in administrative and court proceedings
We do not promise a grant of status. The state decides. Our task is to present your case fully, with evidence and on time.
