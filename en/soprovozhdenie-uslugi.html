Legal assistance

Georgian law for those who live and work here

Alongside our core Interpol and international protection practice, we handle everyday legal matters: contracts, disputes, business, property and family cases. Our clients have moved to Georgia or run affairs here remotely.

Cases are handled by Georgian advocates, with communication in Russian or English. Fees are named after a free consultation; retainer arrangements are available for companies.

Typical requests

  • Reviewing a contract before signing
  • Registering a company or sole proprietorship
  • A property transaction
  • A dispute with a landlord, employer or seller
  • Inheritance, divorce, maintenance
Practice areas

Legal services

Consultations

Georgian civil and criminal law: inheritance, marriage and divorce, maintenance, consumer rights, intellectual property.

Review of documents, leases, employment and commercial contracts. We explain the risks and propose changes.

Complaints, claims, statements of claim and responses under Georgian procedure.

Company or sole proprietor registration, bank accounts, ongoing corporate matters, retainer service.

Checks on the property and the seller, support with the sale and registration of ownership.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need to speak Georgian?

No. We work in Russian or English and arrange document translations ourselves.

Can you work remotely?

Yes. Most work is done online, and with a power of attorney we can represent you without your presence.

Do you work with companies?

Yes, including on retainer: contracts, corporate matters and disputes.

The first consultation is free. We first understand the situation and tell you honestly whether it is worth taking on.
