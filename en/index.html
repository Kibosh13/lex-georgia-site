Questions

Frequently asked questions

How can I find out if I am wanted by Interpol?

We prepare an official request to the Commission for the Control of Files to confirm or rule out a notice.

Do you guarantee removal from the database?

Decisions of state and international bodies are outside our control. Our responsibility is a professional approach, transparency at every stage and a legal strategy built around the facts of your case.

How long does Interpol take to consider a deletion request?

Up to 9 months.

What does deletion from Interpol’s database give you?

The chance to live freely, plan ahead and cross borders without the legal risks tied to an Interpol notice.

Can I instruct you if I am abroad?

Yes. Where you are is not an obstacle. We handle matters remotely, with convenient communication, prompt document exchange and confidentiality at every stage.
