Questions

Frequently asked

How can I find out if I am wanted by Interpol?

You may request access to data about yourself from Interpol’s Commission for the Control of Files (CCF). We help draft that request.

Do you guarantee removal from the database?

No. The Commission decides. We do not promise an outcome — we prepare the case and represent you.

Do I need to visit the office?

No. Consultations and most of the work are online. Office: Tbilisi, Andria Razmadze Street 68. Meetings by appointment, 10:00–20:00.

How much do you charge?

We do not publish prices. Fees are named after the free consultation and set out in the agreement.
