Who it is for

When a claim is worth preparing

A person may apply if rights at home have been violated so seriously that return is unsafe. Typical grounds are persecution for political opinion, religion, nationality or membership of a social group, including sexual orientation.

We also look at criminal cases that appear to be a form of pressure. A case file does not by itself close the door: what matters is the facts and the evidence.

What we assemble

  • A timeline in your own words and in the documents
  • Decisions, summonses, medical and other records
  • Translations for the procedure
  • A list of what is still missing before filing
The work

From assessment to a decision

Assessment

Before filing we test whether there are grounds and where the account is weak.

Application

We draft the claim to Georgia’s migration authority and the annexes.

Interview

We go through likely questions and attend the interview.

A refusal

If the decision is negative, we look at whether an appeal is worth bringing.

Can I apply if there is a criminal case or a notice?

Yes, the question of protection can still be raised. The authority looks at whether there is persecution and how it is proved, not only at the wording of a charge.

Is a refusal the end?

No. A first-instance refusal can be appealed. After that we look at whether any new facts actually change the case.

We do not promise status. The state grants it. Our work is to build the file, meet the procedure and say plainly where the case is weak.
