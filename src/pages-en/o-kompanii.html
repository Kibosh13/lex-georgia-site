About the firm

Our core work is Interpol notice removal. The office is in Tbilisi; we assist clients from any country.

What we do

ITP Consulting provides comprehensive work on challenging Interpol and UN notices: data checks, CCF filings, support in extradition requests and work on refusals to remove a notice. The firm also assists with international protection, relocation and residence permits in Georgia, the European Union and Latin America.

Our team includes advocates with extensive experience in this specific field.
