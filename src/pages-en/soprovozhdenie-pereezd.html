Turnkey relocation

One coordinator for every relocation task

Moving means dozens of tasks at once: housing, banking, phone, documents, schools. We take them on so that your first weeks in Georgia go smoothly.

Support can be ordered on its own or together with legal protection, for example when the move is linked to a notice or an asylum claim. The package is tailored to you and your family.

What we take on

  • Airport meeting and transfer on arrival
  • Phone and bank account
  • Short-term and long-term housing
  • Schools, kindergartens, doctors
  • Residence permit and legal matters

Questions

Common questions

Can we start before moving?

Yes. Housing search, document preparation and residence advice can begin remotely.

Do you only work in Tbilisi?

Mainly Tbilisi; by arrangement we also help in Batumi and Kutaisi.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the package and family size. We name the fee after a free consultation.
