How to find out if you are in Interpol’s files

How a request to the Commission for the Control of Files works and what to expect.

Many people learn about a notice only at a border or a bank. You can check earlier through Interpol’s Commission for the Control of Files (CCF), an independent body that reviews individual requests.

Who may apply

Anyone may request access to data about themselves processed in Interpol’s information system. You may apply in person or through a representative with a power of attorney.

What you need

  • a completed request form in one of Interpol’s working languages;
  • a copy of an identity document;
  • a power of attorney if a representative applies.

What to expect

The Commission says whether data exist if the source country does not object to disclosure. In some cases the reply is limited. That is why the request must be drafted carefully, and the next steps thought through in advance.

This note is for information only and is not a consultation. Each case needs its own review.
