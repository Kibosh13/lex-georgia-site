Residence permit

Which ground fits your situation

Countries grant residence permits on several grounds: employment, business, study, family, property or investment. Each has its own requirements for documents, income and term. Choosing the wrong ground is the most common reason for refusal.

At the consultation we review your situation and choose the ground with the best prospects and the simplest preparation. If a permit has already been refused, we analyse the decision and advise whether to appeal or reapply.

Grounds

Types of residence permit in Georgia

We use Georgia as an example. Residence permits can also be obtained in European Union and Latin American countries.

Employment

Work under a contract with a Georgian company. The company must show turnover, as a guide GEL 50,000 per year for each foreign employee. Issued for up to a year and renewable.

Business

For business owners and individual entrepreneurs. Turnover, no tax debts and real activity matter. Issued for up to a year and renewable.

Study

For students of accredited universities and schools. You need proof of enrolment and funds for the whole period. Renewed for the duration of studies.

Family

For spouses, children and parents of a Georgian citizen or a foreigner with a permit. Proof of kinship and funds is required. The term follows the relative’s status.

Property

For owners of non-agricultural real estate valued at USD 100,000 or more. Issued for a year and renewable while you own the property.

Investment

For investments of USD 300,000 or more in the Georgian economy or property. Issued for 5 years, after which you may apply for permanent residence.

Questions

Common questions about residence

How long does the review take?

Usually up to 30 days, faster for an extra fee. Time to gather documents depends on the ground and what you already have.

Can I get a permit without coming to Georgia?

Filing and collecting the card usually require you to be present. Document preparation can start remotely.

Can my family get permits too?

Yes, a spouse and children can obtain permits on family grounds after the main applicant.

What if the permit is refused?

A refusal can be challenged administratively and in court, or you can reapply after fixing the reason. We decide after reviewing the specific refusal.

Requirements change. Figures and conditions are given as a guide. We confirm the current requirements for your case at the consultation.
